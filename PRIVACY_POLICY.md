# Privacy Policy for Zingerr

**Last Updated:** May 11, 2026

## 1. Introduction

Awadh Info Solution Pvt Ltd ("Company," "we," "us," or "our") operates the Zingerr mobile application ("App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our App.

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. If you do not agree with our policies and practices, please do not use our App.

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Information You Provide Directly

**Phone Number and OTP Authentication:**
- We collect your phone number to send you One-Time Passwords (OTPs) for authentication and account verification purposes
- OTPs are temporary codes used to verify your identity and secure your account
- Your phone number is essential for account creation and login

**Account Information:**
- Name
- Email address
- Profile picture (optional)
- Address and location information (optional)

**Communication Data:**
- Messages, feedback, and support tickets you send to us

### 2.2 Information Collected Automatically

**Device Information:**
- Device type, model, and operating system
- Device identifiers and hardware identifiers
- Mobile network information
- Device settings and configurations

**Usage Information:**
- Pages or features accessed
- Time and duration of activities
- Crash reports and error logs
- Performance metrics

**Location Information:**
- With your permission, we may collect precise GPS location data
- General location based on IP address

## 3. Use of Your Information

We use the collected information for:

- **Authentication & Security:** OTP generation and verification for secure account access
- **Service Provision:** Delivering, maintaining, and improving the App
- **Communication:** Sending you updates, notifications, and support messages
- **Analytics:** Understanding user behavior and improving user experience
- **Legal Compliance:** Fulfilling legal obligations and preventing fraud
- **Customer Support:** Responding to inquiries and resolving issues
- **Personalization:** Tailoring content and recommendations to your preferences

## 4. OTP (One-Time Password) Policy

### 4.1 OTP Collection and Use
- OTPs are sent to your registered phone number for authentication purposes only
- OTPs expire after a short period (typically 10 minutes)
- You will never be charged for OTP messages; however, your carrier's standard messaging rates may apply

### 4.2 OTP Security
- OTPs are encrypted and transmitted securely
- Never share your OTP with anyone, including company representatives
- We never ask for your OTP via email or phone calls

### 4.3 OTP Data Retention
- OTP records are retained for security and compliance purposes for up to 90 days
- After which, they are securely deleted

## 5. Sharing of Information

We do **NOT** sell, trade, or rent your personal information. We may share information only in these cases:

- **Third-Party Service Providers:** SMS providers and cloud service providers (subject to confidentiality agreements)
- **Legal Requirements:** When required by law or to protect our rights
- **Business Transfers:** In case of merger, acquisition, or bankruptcy
- **User Consent:** With your explicit consent

## 6. Data Security

We implement comprehensive security measures including:

- **Encryption:** SSL/TLS encryption for data in transit
- **Secure Storage:** Encrypted storage for sensitive data
- **Access Controls:** Limited access to personal information
- **Regular Audits:** Security assessments and vulnerability testing
- **PCI DSS Compliance:** If applicable, for payment information

However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 7. Your Privacy Rights

You have the right to:

- **Access:** Request a copy of your personal data
- **Correction:** Update or correct inaccurate information
- **Deletion:** Request deletion of your data (subject to legal requirements)
- **Opt-Out:** Withdraw consent for certain data processing
- **Data Portability:** Request your data in a portable format

To exercise these rights, contact us at support@awadhinfosolution.in

## 8. Data Retention

- **OTP Records:** Retained for 90 days for security purposes
- **Account Data:** Retained as long as your account is active
- **Communication Records:** Retained for 2 years or as required by law
- **Marketing Data:** Retained until you unsubscribe

Upon account deletion, your data will be securely deleted within 30 days, except where retention is required by law.
