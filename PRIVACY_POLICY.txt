Last Updated: May 11, 2026

1. INTRODUCTION

Awadh Info Solution Pvt Ltd ("Company," "we," "us," or "our") operates the Zingerr mobile application ("App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our App. Please read this Privacy Policy carefully before using the App.

2. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

2.1 INFORMATION YOU PROVIDE DIRECTLY

Phone Number and OTP Authentication:
- We collect your phone number to send you One-Time Passwords (OTPs) for authentication and account verification
- OTPs are temporary codes used to verify your identity and secure your account
- Your phone number is required for account creation and login

Account Information:
- Name
- Email address
- Profile picture (optional)
- Address and location information (optional)

Communication Data:
- Messages, feedback, and support requests you send to us

2.2 INFORMATION COLLECTED AUTOMATICALLY

Device Information:
- Device type, model, and operating system
- Device identifiers
- Mobile network information
- Device settings

Usage Information:
- Pages and features accessed
- Time and duration of activities
- Crash reports and error logs
- Performance metrics

Location Information:
- GPS location data (with your permission)
- General location based on IP address

3. HOW WE USE YOUR INFORMATION

We use your information for:
- Authentication and account security via OTP verification
- Delivering and improving the App services
- Sending updates, notifications, and support messages
- Understanding user behavior and enhancing user experience
- Complying with legal obligations
- Preventing fraud and ensuring security
- Providing customer support
- Personalizing content and recommendations

4. OTP (ONE-TIME PASSWORD) POLICY

OTP Collection and Use:
- OTPs are sent to your registered phone number for authentication only
- OTPs expire automatically (typically within 10 minutes)
- You will not be charged for OTPs; however, your carrier's standard messaging rates may apply

OTP Security:
- OTPs are encrypted and securely transmitted
- Never share your OTP with anyone
- We never ask for OTPs via email or phone calls

OTP Data Retention:
- OTP records are retained for 90 days for security and compliance
- After this period, records are securely deleted

5. SHARING OF YOUR INFORMATION

We do NOT sell, trade, or rent your personal information. We may share information in these limited cases:
- With SMS and cloud service providers (under confidentiality agreements)
- When required by law or legal process
- In case of business transfers, mergers, or bankruptcy
- With your explicit consent

6. DATA SECURITY

We implement security measures including:
- SSL/TLS encryption for data transmission
- Encrypted storage for sensitive information
- Access controls and limited permissions
- Regular security audits and testing
- Secure deletion of data when no longer needed

You have the right to:
